But then, a director shouted "Cut!" — they were actually on a film set in Hollywood! Four actors didn't come that morning, so the children helped act in the movie. Jack wasn't very good at acting, but Sofia told him he was a great actor. At the end, everyone said the movie was great. Value: Encourage your friends.
